Support Eden

The alliance website, the event rankings and the alliance sheet are run by volunteers from the guilds. If they've helped you, you can chip in on Ko-fi.

Support Eden on Ko-fi

What your support pays for

  • The edenafk.com domain and anything else the sites need to stay online.
  • Keeping the guild directory, expedition maps and event rankings up to date.

Support is a thank-you, never expected, and doesn't buy anything in the game or in the alliance. Eden isn't a registered charity, so it isn't tax-deductible. On Ko-fi you can keep your name and message private.

Other ways to help

Expedition data is entered by hand by a member of each guild. If you'd like to help, contact IDKTrout on Discord, or come say hi at discord.gg/joineden.
